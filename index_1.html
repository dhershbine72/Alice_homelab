ALICE HOMELAB
Privacy

Personal automation — single operator

One person's home-run automation, for his own accounts.

Alice Homelab is a set of personal scripts and scheduled tools built and operated by a single individual, Andrew, entirely for his own use: a YouTube content pipeline for his own channels, plus Tasks, Calendar, and Gmail organization tools for his own personal Google account. It is not a public product, a service anyone can sign up for, or a company — it exists to automate one person's own accounts and workflow.

Operator
Andrew (sole account holder)
Scope
Andrew's own Google accounts only
Public signup
none — not offered
Runs
Scheduled, on private infrastructure

What it does

  • Generates serialized fiction episodes and assembles narration, visuals, and captions into finished video files.
  • Uploads and manages those videos on Andrew's own YouTube channels via the YouTube Data API.
  • Lists, adds, and checks off items in Andrew's own Google Tasks.
  • Reads Andrew's own Google Calendar (and, for a planned feature not yet built, will create or modify events on it).
  • Reads, labels, and archives messages in Andrew's own personal Gmail inbox to keep it organized.
  • Sends Andrew status and failure notifications for runs he isn't watching live.

Why this page exists

Google's OAuth verification requires a public homepage and privacy policy for any app requesting account access, even a private, single-user tool like this one. This page and its privacy policy exist to satisfy that requirement — see the privacy policy for exactly what data each part of this project touches and why.
